My Income Secure (myincomesecure.com) is operated by Ascendant Benefits LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what information we collect through this site and how we use it.

Information you provide

The Benefit Estimator tool on this site asks for your state, approximate annual income, and monthly living expenses. These figures are used only to calculate an on-screen estimate and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers as part of that calculation. If you click "Get covered," your estimate details may be included in an email you choose to send to our team, or passed to our enrollment partner if you continue to sign up for coverage.

If you contact us by email or phone, we retain that correspondence and any information you provide (such as your name, email, phone number, or state) to respond to you and, if you enroll, to service your membership.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) for security and site-performance purposes.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and process enrollment requests
  • To operate, maintain, and improve this site
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you or to request it be deleted, subject to any recordkeeping obligations we have as a program administrator.
